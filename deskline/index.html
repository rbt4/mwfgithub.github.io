Schedule

A distinct amber signal appears in the clock, favicon and installed taskbar badge when a scheduled moment approaches or becomes due.

Pace

Quiet blue sip prompts distribute a 24-ounce target across your configured workday instead of demanding that you finish the bottle all at once.

Timer

Start a 30-, 45- or 60-minute personal timer, walk away, and get a persistent warm signal when it is time to return.
